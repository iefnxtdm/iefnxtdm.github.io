About

I am Director of the R&D Center at COGO LowCarbon Technology in Qianhai, Shenzhen, where I joined in June 2026. I lead research and development in building energy saving and carbon reduction, renewable energy (photovoltaics and energy storage), and AI-integrated building energy management.

Previously, I was a Postdoctoral Researcher at the National Laboratory of the Rockies (NLR), formerly NREL, from February 2024 to May 2026, working on advanced building thermal technologies. I completed my Ph.D. in Civil Engineering at WPI.

Interests: building energy saving and carbon reduction, PV and energy storage, AI-integrated building energy management, dynamic envelopes, thermal switches, SS-PCM, radiative cooling, EnergyPlus co-simulation, and PINN/ML for inverse heat transfer.
